The tile sheets tiles_hand.bmp and tiles_river.bmp

These two pictures are the hand-drawn mahjong tiles of Family mruby's
mahjong app (fmruby-core, flash/app/game/mahjong/), copied here byte for
byte. They are drawn by hand by Katsuhiko Kageyama, the author of Family
mruby and of this gem.

In this gem they are under the MIT License, the license of the gem
(LICENSE), by the author's decision. In fmruby-core they stay under that
repository's license (GPL-3.0).

  tiles_hand.bmp   420x224, 8-bit, cells of 42x56
  tiles_river.bmp  240x128, 8-bit, cells of 24x32

Both are 10 x 4 cells without gaps. Tile n (the table's tile numbers:
0..8 1m..9m, 9 the red 5m, 10..19 the circles likewise, 20..29 the
bamboos, 30..36 east south west north white green red, 37 the back) is at
x = (n % 10) * cell width, y = (n / 10) * cell height. The pixel values
are RGB332 colours and the palette says the same colours; RGB332 0xE3
(magenta) is the transparent colour.
