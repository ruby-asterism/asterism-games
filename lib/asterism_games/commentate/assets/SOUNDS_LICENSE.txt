The sounds in sounds/

These are the sound of Family mruby's mahjong app (fmruby-core,
flash/app/game/mahjong/mahjong_sound.rb), which the commentary page plays:
the effects and jingles, and the BGM of its scenes. They were not played
again by ear: fmruby-core's tools/mahjong_sounds.rb plays the app's notes
and tunes with Family mruby's own APU emulator (components/apu_emu) and
writes the files; sounds/sounds.json lists each file with its SHA-256 and
what it was made from.

     after_the_rain.mp3  "8-bit Chiptune - After The Rain" by shiru8bit
                         https://opengameart.org/content/8-bit-chiptune-after-the-rain
     spring_thing.mp3    "8-bit Chiptune - Spring Thing" by shiru8bit

   The music is not changed. Each file is the original NSF played by
   Family mruby's APU emulator and saved as MP3: from the start of the tune
   through one pass of its loop (and a second more); the page repeats the
   loop as the NSF does. Whoever passes these files on, or plays them in a
   stream or a recording, gives the same credit, for example
   "BGM: shiru8bit (CC BY 3.0)" (the page shows it in its corner).
